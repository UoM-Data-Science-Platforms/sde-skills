Issue: https://github.com/UoM-Data-Science-Platforms/sde-skills/issues/101

Status: planned; implementation pending.

## Scope

- [ ] Include governance as a primary activity where appropriate: AI inventories, accountability, risk and impact assessment, transparency, human oversight, supplier assurance and audit evidence.
- [ ] Reference ISO/IEC 42001 and applicable EU AI Act responsibilities with clear context and authoritative, dated sources.
- [ ] Extend Data Management for training/evaluation data provenance, permissions, transformations, retention and model lineage.
- [ ] Extend Outputs & Disclosure Control for systems supporting review and controlled release of models, embeddings and generated outputs.
- [ ] Extend Projects & Operations for ownership, monitoring, change management, incident response and retirement.
- [ ] Distinguish governance decisions, technical implementation and decisions reserved for authorised specialists. Avoid duplicating infrastructure competencies.

## Acceptance criteria

Responsibilities have clear homes and complete levels in the current YAML model, with governance activities appropriately represented.
