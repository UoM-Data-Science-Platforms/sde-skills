# AI competency scope and sources

Reviewed: 2 November 2026. These references inform the current competency model; they do not establish certification, legal applicability or scientific validity.

## Governance context

- [ISO/IEC 42001:2023, first edition, December 2023](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001) describes an organisational AI management system. It informs accountability, assessment, oversight and assurance activities; it is not an individual qualification or a mandatory prerequisite for these competencies.
- [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689) and the [European Commission AI Act overview](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai), reviewed 2 November 2026, provide the legislative reference and explanatory context. Applicability depends on jurisdiction, organisational role, intended use, risk classification and relevant exceptions. Provider, deployer and general-purpose-model responsibilities differ. Authorised legal specialists must establish applicable obligations and current commencement dates, including amendments; the framework does not assume every research workload is high-risk or exempt.

## Responsibility boundaries

| Domain | Primary responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Technology & Engineering | Operate platforms and implement approved technical controls; provide technical evidence. |
| Governance & Compliance | Maintain inventories, accountability, risk and impact reviews, transparency, human oversight and supplier assurance. Coordinate specialist determinations. |
| Data Management | Maintain data permissions, provenance, transformations, retention and links to model artefacts. |
| Outputs & Disclosure Control | Provide review and release systems for qualified disclosure reviewers. |
| Projects & Operations | Coordinate ownership, monitoring, changes, incidents and retirement. |

Learning-algorithm design and scientific validity remain with researchers or other authorised specialists. Legal, ethics, clinical, disclosure and risk-acceptance decisions remain with those authorised to make them. Platform operation does not confer that authority.
