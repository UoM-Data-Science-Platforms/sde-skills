# Master TTS Register by Domain & Subdomain (Hover-Card Ready)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Hierarchical Multi-Source Sourcing**: This register is organized strictly by **Domain and Subdomain** to preserve context-specific operational roles and allow legitimate duplicate tools/standards across distinct subdomains (e.g. *Python in 2.2 vs 6.1*, *Git in 4.2 vs 6.1*, *HashiCorp Vault in 1.3 vs 2.2*).
> 
> Every item captures all **5 evidence streams** for direct integration into website hover cards:
> 1. **⭐ Community**: Identified in baseline community engagement events.
> 2. **🏛️ National Standards**: Mentions out of the 7 national reference frameworks.
> 3. **🔬 DARE UK Projects**: Utilization in DARE UK funded initiatives (out of 7).
> 4. **💼 Job Postings**: Live UK job ads out of 100 with **Entry (`jr`)**, **Mid (`mid`)**, and **Senior (`snr`)** counts.
> 5. **📊 Industry Radars**: Position across 4 technology radars with adoption status.
