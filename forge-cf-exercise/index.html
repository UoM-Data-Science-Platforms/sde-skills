SDE Forge Competency Framework Exercise

Test out our A.I. agent skill, explore the Competency Framework, and report back on your experience.

Overview

In this exercise, we are asking you to navigate to the A.I. Agent Skill page and put it to the test. Your goal is to interact with the agent, explore its capabilities using the Competency Framework, and then tell us about your experience.

Privacy & Safety Guidelines

Before you begin, please ensure that any personal data you upload to the A.I. agent is carefully anonymised.

  • Remove Contact Details: Strip out your name, email address, phone number, and physical address.
  • Remove Identifying Locations: Please remove references to your high school or secondary school, as well as any highly specific identifiable locations.
  • Redact Specific Achievements: Remove highly unique identifiable attributes (e.g., specific awards or ultra-niche projects that could easily identify you).
  • Mock C.V. Option: If you are uncomfortable using your own C.V., you can optionally use the mock C.V. we have provided to test the agent's capabilities.

Instructions & Hints

To get the best out of the A.I. agent, tailor your prompt to your goals based on what the agent is designed to do.

1. Choose a Persona

Select a persona or use case that applies most to you and your career objectives:

  • Aspiring Builders: You are a recent graduate, have some technical skills from personal or work projects, an internship, or an apprenticeship, or you are looking to transition career tracks from an adjacent field such as data analysis or data engineering.
  • Experienced RTPs: You already work developing SDEs or have a technical IT role and you want to upskill, understand your current experience level, or plan for a promotion.
  • Team Leads: You want to understand your team's capabilities and experience levels to assess the overall health of the team, understand skills gaps, recognise capacity risks, plan for building capacity, generate a job specification, or review C.V.s submitted for a job posting.

2. What to Ask

Try prompting the agent with tasks related to your chosen persona:

  • Aspiring Builders: Share your anonymised C.V. or background. Ask the agent to outline the foundational skills you need to transition into an SDE role and what you should focus on learning first.
  • Experienced RTPs: Paste your anonymised C.V. and ask the agent to map your current experience against the framework, identify your level, and suggest concrete objectives for a promotion.
  • Team Leads: Paste a draft Job Description to see if it aligns with the framework, or describe your team's current projects to have the agent identify potential skills gaps and capacity risks.

Troubleshooting

Sometimes A.I. agents can be stubborn or may not follow their system instructions perfectly. Here are some mitigations if you run into issues.

Agent Won't Fetch Data

The agent's system instructions tell it to fetch the framework data from our website using YAML files. However, some LLM providers might refuse to access external URLs or tell you they cannot read live websites.

Solution: If the agent fails to fetch the framework data automatically, you can manually provide the data. Navigate to the SDE Skills GitHub repository, find the relevant YAML files for the framework domains, and simply copy and paste the text content directly into the chat.
